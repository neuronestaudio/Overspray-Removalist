### Cement Splatter Removal

Building sites are often a cause of damage. Concrete fallout caused by concrete pumping or spraying, concreted dust and cutting can cause all types of issues for vehicle owners and builders. Concrete is a mixture of several ingredients such as gravel or crushed stone, cement and sand that can cause serious damage to a paint finish. This is the reason, the process to remove concrete from the car exterior is a chemical one and not a mechanical one. Any attempt to remove the concrete mechanically may result in permanent damage to the paint. The only safe way to remove the concrete splatter is to change the state of the concrete.

There are now several chemical products that can remove splatter without harming the operator or the paint film.

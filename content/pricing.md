## About Us

### Pricing

Our best way to price a problem is by viewing the vehicle but we can most definitely give estimates over the phone. Emailing images to us will give us a much better understanding of a problem and knowing the type of fallout it is.

#### Overspray Paint

When quoting an overspray issue on a vehicle it can depend on many different factors which will determine the price to repair the problem. In some cases the paint may only be very light and not cover the entire vehicle this may cost from $600. Different types of paint can bond differently to the vehicle which can change the process needed to remove the overspray. Two pack epoxy is a very hard paint which is cured through adding a hardener, if the car is fully covered including the molds, plastics, glass, window trims, lenses, roof racks and all external accessories the vehicle has to be sited and assessed.

If there are a high volume amount of cars effected in one place and we can work on the entire lot of vehicles in the one location then we can give a price on a volume lot.

We can offer a service which includes authorisation and release forms and handle the public relations on your behalf.

#### Cement splatter

Vehicles that have been effected by cement or concrete splatter will depend on many different factors which will determine the price to repair the problem. If the car is fully covered including the moulds, plastics, glass, window trims, lenses, roof racks and all external accessories and especially if lime staining occurs after the cement is removed off the duco we can deal with the situation and the vehicles will need to be sited and assessed.

Our biggest clients are construction companies when they are pouring their slabs. Concrete splatter travels over the edge of the building site onto vehicles below.

#### Other types of fallout

The same applies to most types of fallout, acid rain, industrial fallout, chemical fallout, all these problems effect vehicles differently. These vehicles need to be sited and evaluated.

### Other Services We Provide

#### Paint Overspray Removal

Paint overspray is usually caused by spray painting, but also roller painting and brush painting. Poor filtration systems in spray booths is also a considering factor. In most cases, this damage is accidentally caused by painting outdoors, due to wind changes or high altitude work. The air-borne emissions can travel up to five hundred metres in windy conditions and cause widespread damage to your vehicle

#### Cement Splatter Removal

Building sites are often a cause of damage. Concrete fallout caused by concrete pumping or spraying, concreted dust and cutting can cause all types of issues for vehicle owners and definitely the builders. Our proven methods and technics have serviced numerous amount of damaged vehicles with great success leaving the customer “impressed and satisfied” with the outcome.

#### Graffiti Removal

Malicious or vandalism damage is a common occurrence. Whether spray can graffiti or a tin of paint thrown onto a vehicle can be a disturbing experience for the owner of the vehicle. The Overspray Removalist has the experience to take care of the problem and bring the vehicle back to its original state.

#### Acid Rain and Environmental Damage

The Overspray Removalists are specialists in dealing with issues of chemical and acid rain fallout. This is usually caused by faulty industrial filtration systems, industrial explosions, factory fires or pollution in the atmosphere. Steel dust or metal fallout can be a problem with the friction that trains or trams generate on their steel tracks.
